# Code of Conduct

This project is a friendly place. Everyone participating — in issues, pull
requests, and discussions — is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and welcome newcomers.
- Keep conversations on-topic and free of harassment, discrimination, and
  spam.

Unacceptable behaviour may be removed by the maintainer, and repeat offenders
may be blocked from the project. If you experience or witness a problem,
please contact the maintainer privately (see SECURITY.md for the private
reporting channel).

This is a short adaptation of the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
