> **Prasyarat:** S06 lulus · **Spec:** `00-design.md` §13 · `05-design-system.md`, `06-frontend.md`

tanpa satu pun emoji, gradient, atau pill dekoratif.

- Create: `crates/hq-server/build.rs`
- Test: `web/src/lib/components/*.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/shell.spec.ts`
